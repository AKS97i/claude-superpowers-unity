# Vision

> The first place a Unity developer — solo or AAA — turns when they want an expert second brain.

## The one-sentence vision

**Unity Debugger** aims to be the definitive, community-trusted library of AI Engineering Systems that give an AI assistant the judgment of a senior Unity engineering team, across the entire game-development lifecycle.

## What "trusted AI resource" means here

Trust is earned through **consistency, honesty, and accuracy**, not breadth alone:

- **Consistency** — the same problem yields the same disciplined methodology every time, because the methodology is explicit and encoded.
- **Honesty** — every diagnosis or recommendation carries a confidence level and says what would raise it. We would rather say "Medium confidence, here's how to confirm" than bluff.
- **Accuracy** — guidance reflects how Unity actually behaves: real APIs, Editor windows, log files, lifecycle order, serialization rules, IL2CPP realities, and version-specific quirks.

## Who it serves

Everyone who ships with Unity:

- **Solo & indie developers** who need a senior engineer on call.
- **Studio teams** who need consistent methodology, review rigor, and onboarding leverage.
- **Technical leads** who want repeatable engineering processes encoded and shared.

## What success looks like

- A Unity developer installs one Superpower and immediately gets help that feels like pairing with a specialist.
- Studios standardize on these systems for debugging, review, and performance work.
- The catalog grows through community contribution while the quality bar holds.
- The repository becomes a reference for *how to build AI engineering systems*, not just a place to download prompts.

## What this is **not**

- ❌ A Unity plugin, package, asset, or game.
- ❌ A pile of one-off prompts.
- ❌ A place for untested, "looks plausible" advice.

It is a library of **engineering systems** — see [philosophy.md](philosophy.md).

## North-star principle

> If we cannot write an evaluation for a Superpower, it is not ready to ship.

Measurable quality is the moat. See [testing-superpowers.md](testing-superpowers.md).
